# Brief: example role

The brief is your instructions for what you want your resume to say, and the only source of
facts for a tailored one. We are not against using AI to write your resume, but it should sound
like you and genuinely represent your experience. Copy this file to `docs/resume-briefs/<id>.md`
for each role and fill it in. Anything not written under "Confirmed experience" must not appear
on the resume.

## Target role

Job title, employer, and a link or paste of the job description.

## JD keywords to match

The skills, tools and themes the job description names, in rough order of importance.

## Positioning

One or two sentences on how you want to read for this role, and what to lead with.

## Strengths to lead with

1. The first strength to put forward, using the wording from the job description.
2. The second.

## Open questions

Facts you are unsure of. Keep them out of the resume until you have confirmed them, then move each into "Confirmed experience".

## Candidate

Your name and the contact details used in the resume header.

## Confirmed experience

Each role, with dates, and the outcomes you can stand behind. Write plain facts: what you did,
what changed, and the evidence. Never invent metrics.

## Gaps

Keywords from the job description that you have little or no experience in, and how to word
anything close to them without stretching the truth.

## Language register

Which spelling to use (for example British or American), the tone, and words to avoid.

## Style

Formatting preferences for this resume, such as how long a bullet may run and which punctuation to avoid.
